I break payment flows before they break for real users.

I'm a Manual Test Engineer working on Uber's risk and payments systems — fraud checks, payment methods, refunds. Every sprint I run through ~500 test cases, dig into edge cases with boundary value analysis and exploratory testing, and usually walk away with 5-6 bugs logged (2-3 of them the kind that would've hurt if they shipped).

Before QA, I was deep in AI/ML — deep learning, CNNs, medical imaging — during internships at NIT Puducherry. That background didn't disappear, it just changed shape. Testing is pattern-recognition and edge-case thinking under a different name, and I bring that lens to how I break things.

## 🎯 What I Actually Do Day to Day

- Execute and maintain test cases across high-risk payment features
- Apply boundary value analysis + exploratory testing to catch what scripted cases miss
- Partner directly with developers to reproduce and verify fixes on P0 issues
- Track everything in JIRA + Zephyr, writing bug reports devs can actually act on without asking me five follow-up questions
